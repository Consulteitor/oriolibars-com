§04How I work

I come in. I analyse and prioritise. Then I leave.

I speak to the people with something useful to say, look at the data that matters and challenge the decisions. I do not build a team around the problem or turn one decision into six months of consulting.

Once it is clear what stays, what stops and what needs deciding, I leave. Your team already knows how to execute.

§06A few decisions I have been involved in

A few decisions I have been involved in

01 · Retention
What it looked like
The business was growing; nothing looked urgent.
What was really happening
Growth was masking a weak customer relationship and a future churn problem.
The decision
Build a loyalty-focused app before the urgency became obvious.
The outcome
Retention moved to the centre of the product decision.
02 · Roadmap
What it looked like
The team needed to deliver every initiative faster.
What was really happening
Capacity was not the issue: too much activity was competing without a shared view of the business.
The decision
Cut the roadmap and align Product, Growth and leadership around one priority.
The outcome
Fewer initiatives. A clearer view of what should move the business.
03 · Ecommerce
What it looked like
More traffic was needed to drive more sales.
What was really happening
Demand was already there; the buying journey was leaving money on the table.
The decision
Fix the buying decision before increasing acquisition.
The outcome
Conversion +196%; online sales grew from 30% to 65%.
